# Data Collection and Usage Disclosure
Effective: {{effective}} · Version 1.0

This page lists every kind of data the app handles, where it goes and why.

## On your phone only
- **Customer list** (names, phone numbers, loan amounts, other columns): kept on your phone to let you call and calculate EMI. Not uploaded.
- **Remarks and follow-ups in full**: kept on your phone. A copy of each call record goes to the server as described below.
- **Settings and EMI history**: kept on your phone.

## Sent to our server (only when your team uses a subscription code)
- **Name and user code**: to know who you are. Required.
- **Random device ID and phone model**: to link the code to one phone. Required.
- **Consent version and time**: proof that you agreed. Required.
- **Call records**: customer name, phone with only the last 4 digits visible, remark, follow-up date and status, attempt number, call time. Used for your dashboard and your Team Leader's dashboard. Required for team use.

## Permissions the app asks for
- **Calling**: to place the call after the countdown. Used only for calls you start.
- **Internet and network state**: to check your access and upload call records.
We do not ask for contacts, SMS, location, microphone, storage or accessibility access.

## Who sees what
- You: your own data.
- Your Team Leader: only their own team.
- The Admin: all records, for support and security.
- Firebase and Render: store and run the service for us.

## What we never do
- Sell data, show ads, share data for marketing, or train AI models with it.
- Record call audio.

## Your choices
Read the User Consent Declaration. You can withdraw consent and delete your data from the More tab.
