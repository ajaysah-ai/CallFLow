# Privacy Policy
Effective: {{effective}} · Version 1.0

{{appName}} ("we", "us") is owned and run by {{owner}}, {{address}}. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and your choices. It is written to follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India.

## Who is who
- **You** are the person using the app (the "User").
- **Your Team Leader** is the person who gave you access, if you joined a team.
- **Your customers** are the people on the lists you import and call.
- For your customers' data, you (or your organisation) decide why and how it is used. We only store it and show it to provide the service.

## What stays on your phone
- The customer lists you import (names, phone numbers, loan amounts and any other columns).
- Your call records, remarks and follow-ups.
We cannot see these unless they are listed in the next section.

## What we collect and store on our server
- Your name, your user code and the team you belong to.
- A random device ID and your phone model, to link your code to one phone.
- Your consent record: the version you accepted and the time.
- Call records: customer name, customer phone number with only the last 4 digits visible, remark, follow-up date, attempt number and call time.
- Last-seen time. Your IP address is used only to block abuse and is not saved in your profile.

## What we do not collect
- Call audio or recordings, your contacts, SMS, location, photos, microphone, or advertising ID.
- Full customer phone numbers and loan amounts.

## Why we use it
- To activate your code and approve your phone.
- To apply your team's subscription and user limit.
- To show your Team Leader and the Admin the team dashboard and reports.
- To protect against misuse and to fix problems.
We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

## Who can see it
- You see your own records.
- Your Team Leader sees only the users and call records of their own team.
- The Admin ({{owner}}) can see all records for support, security and billing.
- Service providers that run our systems: Google Firebase (database) and Render (hosting). They process data only for us.
- Authorities, only when the law requires it.

## Where data is stored
Data is stored on servers of our providers. These may be outside India, for example in Singapore. We use reasonable safeguards.

## How long we keep it

## Children
