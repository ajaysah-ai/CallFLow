# About Us
{{appName}} is a calling assistant for people who call customers every day, such as loan, sales and follow-up teams.

## What it does
- Imports your customer list from CSV or Excel.
- Calls customers one by one, with a short countdown you can pause or stop.
- Asks for a quick remark after every call, with tap-to-add suggestions.
- Keeps follow-ups, history and a personal dashboard.
- Exports your records as CSV or XLSX.
- Gives Team Leaders a dashboard for their own team, and the Admin full control of subscriptions.

## What it does not do
- It does not record calls.
- It does not read your contacts, SMS or location.
- It does not call anyone without your control.
