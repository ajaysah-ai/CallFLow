# User Consent Declaration
Version 1.0 · {{effective}}

By tapping "Agree and continue" you declare that:

- You are 18 years or older.
- You have read the Terms and Conditions, the EULA, the Privacy Policy and the Data Collection and Usage Disclosure.
- You agree that, when you use a subscription code, we store your name, device ID, phone model, consent record and call records (customer name, phone with the last 4 digits visible, remark, follow-up date, time) on our server.
- You agree that your Team Leader can see the call records of users in their team, and the Admin can see all records.
- You agree to the app using the Calling permission to place calls that you start.
- You will call only people you may lawfully call, and follow TRAI and Do Not Disturb rules.
- You have the right to use the customer data you import.

## Withdrawing consent
You can withdraw consent at any time: More tab, then "Delete everything". This deletes your data on the phone and on the server, ends your access and returns the app to this consent screen. You can also write to {{email}}.
Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did before you withdrew.
