# Data Deletion Policy
Effective: {{effective}} · Version 1.0

You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

## How to delete
- **In the app:** More tab, then "Delete everything". This deletes your records on the phone and on our server, frees your seat in the team, and withdraws your consent. You need internet for the server part.
- **Phone only:** More tab, then "Delete all data". This deletes the lists and records on the phone but not on the server.
- **By request:** write to {{email}} from the contact you gave us, with your name and user code. If your access is closed and the app cannot delete for you, use this way.
- **Team Leaders** can remove users of their team. Removing a user deletes that user's call records from the server immediately.
- **Admin** can delete a Team Leader. This deletes all users and call records of that team immediately.

## What is deleted
- Your user record, code, device link and consent record.
- All your call records on the server.
- Data on your phone, when you use the in-app options.

## How long it takes
In-app and dashboard deletions happen at once. Requests by email are completed within {{deletionDays}} days. Backups, if any, are deleted within 30 days after that.

## What may be kept
We may keep the minimum data needed to follow the law, to settle a dispute, or to prevent fraud. If we keep anything, we tell you.

## Customer data
If a customer asks to be removed, tell the User or the Team Leader who called them. You can delete that customer from your list. You can also write to {{email}}.
